    A .mindpack is one memory: its findings with quotes, notes, history, recipe and (usually) its material. Nothing is changed until you confirm, and an update keeps the previous version so you can roll back.
    New fusion

    A fusion is a new memory made from the selected ones. It reads their material together and dreams on what lies between them: patterns that only appear when they're read side by side. The memories it's made from stay as they are.
