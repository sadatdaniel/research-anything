What happened

    New skill preview

    Reading your own files comes in Phase 7. Until then, skills are made from the fake chronicles on the command line.

    Import a mindpack

    A .mindpack is one skill: its findings with quotes, notes, history, recipe and (usually) its material. Nothing is changed until you confirm, and an update keeps the previous version so you can roll back.

    Combine and learn preview

    A new skill made from the selected ones. It keeps every finding they already have, then dreams on what lies between them: patterns that only appear when their material is read together.

    Training a combined skill comes with the skill manager (Phase 7). Compare and Ask already work across skills.
